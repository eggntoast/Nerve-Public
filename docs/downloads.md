[All files and updates on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/nerve/files/all)

Do not combine the TBC and Forever packages. GitHub’s **Code → Download ZIP** contains this gallery and its help pages, not an installable addon.

## Current support

Both versions support Warrior’s **Execute, Overpower and Revenge**. TBC has separate Arms, Fury and Protection setups; Forever keeps per-character settings.

**Victory Rush in WoW Forever still needs in-game testing.** It is not included in the supported-ability list above.

The plan is to add the other classes one at a time until all nine are supported.
